# Global-Electronics-Sales-Performance-Analysis(Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing global electronics sales data (2016–2021) across countries, product categories, brands, and individual products — built to uncover revenue trends, seasonal patterns, and performance drivers.

## Overview

This project simulates a real-world business intelligence reporting scenario for a global electronics retailer. The dashboard consolidates sales, customer, and product data into a single interactive report, enabling stakeholders to quickly identify what's driving revenue, where it's coming from, and where performance is lagging.

## Objectives

- Consolidate multi-year sales data into a single source of truth for reporting
- Identify revenue trends, seasonality, and year-over-year growth patterns
- Break down performance by country, product category, and brand
- Surface top- and bottom-performing products to guide inventory and marketing decisions
- Present findings in a clear, stakeholder-ready format

## Dataset

- Multi-year global electronics sales data (2016–2021)
- Fields include: orders, order dates, quantities, revenue, product categories, product names, brands, countries, and customer records

## Process

1. **Data Collection & Preprocessing** — Consolidated raw sales, product, and customer data from multiple tables.
2. **Data Cleaning** — Handled missing values, removed duplicates, and standardized formatting across revenue, orders, quantity, and customer fields.
3. **Data Modeling** — Structured relationships between sales, products, and geography tables to support accurate aggregation and drill-down analysis.
4. **Analysis** — Explored trends, patterns, and anomalies in revenue performance, seasonality, and category/brand contribution.
5. **Dashboard Development** — Built interactive Power BI report pages with KPIs, charts, and drill-through views.

## Dashboard Pages

**1. Executive Summary**
- Key KPIs: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Total Customers, Average Order Value
- Revenue & YoY growth trend (2016–2021)
- Revenue by country and by category

**2. Product & Brand Performance**
- Top and bottom performing products by category
- Quantity sold by category
- Brand-level revenue contribution and ranking

**3. Country & Category Deep Dive**
- Revenue and quantity by country
- Category-level breakdown: quantity %, revenue rank, total profit, and profit margin %

## Key Insights

- Total revenue of **$55.7M** was generated across **62,884 units** sold and **~15K customers**.
- Revenue peaked in **2019 at $18.3M**, followed by a sharp decline in 2020–21 coinciding with COVID-19 disruption.
- Revenue consistently peaks in **December and February** each year, while **March and April** are the weakest months.
- The **United States** is the dominant market, contributing **53.5%** of total revenue; the remaining seven countries combined contribute the rest.
- **Computers** and **Home Appliances** together account for **53.9%** of total revenue, with Computers alone generating **$19.3M**.
- Three brands — **Adventure Works, Contoso, and Wide World Importers** — account for over **57%** of total revenue; Adventure Works and Wide World Importers lead in Computers, while Contoso leads in Home Appliances.
- Category-level profit margins are fairly consistent, ranging from **17–19%**, with Music, Movies & Audio Books posting the highest margin at 19.27%.
